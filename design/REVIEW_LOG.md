# Design review log

## Round 1 (Player, Builder, Art & sound director → Refiner)

### Findings


Severity tags as given by the reviewer. Numbers are the reviewers' claims: verify before acting.

## Economy and win conditions
- P1/B1 (must-fix): Regrowth r=2.0 returns a flattened cell to h≥0.05 in ~26 ms and 0.95 in 1.5 s; so (a) the gold trail vanishes almost immediately (contradicts "slowly grow back"), (b) the full-clear win (`every valid cell h<0.05 && toughness<=0`) is unreachable by hand, and the HUD "% cleared" would hover near 0, (c) re-scrubbing one patch earns near-full energy: frantic scribbling at ~80-150 cells/s banks L15's 658 threshold in ~5-10 s vs the 2.5-3.5 min target; the speed bonus (+50%) rewards haste. Suggest: slow, truly logistic regrowth from a seed, e.g. `h += r·max(h,0.05)·(1-h)·dt`, r≈0.1-0.3/s (10-30 s recovery); energy only for grass above a height, e.g. `max(0,h-0.3)/0.7`; speed bonus cap ~+20%; define "cleared" as ≥~97% of valid cells below ~0.15 (or "swept at least once this field"), excluding cells under bugs; one main progress readout (meter); keep "slows for grass swept in the last second".
- P2/B2 (must-fix/should-fix): Full Clear (flat 75) pays out ~2x threshold and simultaneously meets full-clear → instant win + 20% carry-over; from ~L10, carry-over alone ≥75 so the next field is won in its first second; time-bonus scoring rewards it. Ring (10) flattens ~29 cells ≈ 32 energy; Line (20) along 36 cells flattens ~180. All moves net-positive; Line ~5x sweeping. Moves skip cells under insects so "cleared" with bugs is impossible via moves → carry-over only possible on bug-free fields. Suggest: moves flatten but grant no (or small) energy — they serve the coverage win; price as % of threshold (e.g. Ring 4%, Line 8%, Clear 35%) or scale with field; rule "no move combo wins a field in under N seconds" as a unit test; carry-over only on "Next field", not replays.
- P4 (should-fix): Petting can be spammed (+5 energy, +10 score, petted 0.8 s, hearts at 1/5/15 pets reachable in ~12 s). Suggest: energy/score once per bug per field; extra taps still hop/hearts/chirp; hearts count fields where you befriended it, not taps.
- P5 (should-fix): Arming deducts the cost up-front so the win meter drops — reads as going backward; hints explain how, not why. Suggest: preview expected effect at the cursor while armed; deduct only when the move fires (removes refund edge cases on cancel/pause/field end); consider sizing Ring/Line with the field.

## Phones and input
- P3/B3/A2 (must-fix/should-fix): On 390×844 portrait a 36-wide field gives ≤10.8 px cells (landscape 844×390 ~10 px; desktop 1280×800 ~17 px), not "14 to 40 px". Bugs at 1 cell = 10 px smudges; pet target 10 px vs the 48 px rule; anger radius 1.5 cells ≈ 15 px so the red ring is hidden under the thumb → unseen bites; a 1-cell Bresenham stroke is a 10 px line under a 40 px finger; tap limits 200 ms/10 px misclassify relaxed taps (slow tap on ladybug nudges it away). Suggest: size in CSS px: bugs drawn max(28 px, 1.8 cells) (A: 30-36 px; 128 px sprites cover 3x DPR); pet hit radius ≥24 px; anger radius max(1.5 cells, 36 px), ring drawn ≥48 px, plus buzz + haptic tick, and an off-field warning (meter edge tint or "!" offset from the touch); brush radius ~18-22 px touch, ~12 px mouse (≥1 cell) stamped along the path; tap limits 350 ms / 14 px; retune energy after.
- B4 (must-fix): No `touch-action: none` on the field and no `overscroll-behavior: none` (verified: style.css has neither); a drag starts a browser pan → pointercancel ends the stroke; Chrome Android pull-to-refresh; iOS long-press callout. No handling of pointercancel/lostpointercapture/blur/visibilitychange → stuck-down pointer keeps a hostile angry and biting, armed Line never fires/refunds, rustle keeps playing. Suggest: touch-action none, overscroll none, user-select none, -webkit-touch-callout none, setPointerCapture; treat cancel/lostcapture/blur as pointer-up (end stroke, anger → wander, cancel+refund armed Line, stop rustle); auto-pause on visibilitychange; e2e touch drag on WebKit iPhone 12 asserting cells along the whole path flattened.
- P6 (should-fix): Desktop is hold-and-drag for minutes; tiring on trackpads. Suggest a "Sweep by hovering" setting for mouse (click still pets/fires moves; "fresh stroke" for stiff = pointer left the field or rested ≥0.5 s); state that only a sweeping pointer angers biters.
- P7/B (should-fix): Loosened stiff grass (toughness 0) has no look of its own → players can't see what's left; zig-zag doesn't count. Suggest a "loosened stiff" atlas variant (bent, outline gone), haptic on loosening, hint "Loosen it, then sweep again", rate-limit the twang.

## Rendering
- B8 (should-fix): The grass renderer spec leaves hard choices open: bend should be skewX/rotation about the tuft base driven by a decaying per-cell bend vector (not an offset); colour driven by a per-cell `goldness` (1 on touch, fades with regrowth) separate from height, or say it's height; tufts taller than the cell spill → draw in row order back-to-front; motes flying to a DOM meter need one full-screen canvas (backdrop, field, particles) with DOM HUD/toolbar over it; atlas at 40 px × dpr 3 × 330 images ≈ 19 MB → build at actual cell size, cap dpr 2.
- A1 (should-fix): Code-drawn grass (most of the screen) isn't tied to the painted look; the ladybug is a weak reference for landscapes; the green-to-gold atlas isn't graded per time of day so it'll look pasted onto amber golden hour. Suggest: the first nanogen surroundings (morning) becomes a second "scene anchor" referenced (with the ladybug) by every other nanogen image; generate one painterly tuft per grass type with imagegen2 (~256 px transparent, ref anchors) as the atlas base or as the code painter's reference; take atlas colours from the palette/scene anchor and apply a per-time-of-day grade to the atlas at build.
- A2 (should-fix): Colour clashes: green caterpillar on green grass; yellow bee/wasp on gold swept grass. Suggest caterpillar non-green (orange with blue spots or cream with dark stripes); bold readable silhouette, strong dark ink outline, high contrast in the style line; drop shadows; distinguish grass types by silhouette (tall wisps, short stubble, flowered dots, stiff straight dark spikes) and test on a 10 px grid early.

## Testability
- B6 (should-fix): Only grass types are seeded; spawns and wander use Math.random; regrowth, anger and bites run on wall-clock rAF → a recorded path won't replay identically, so "every field can be completed" can't be proven reliably. ## Round 2 (Player, Builder, Art & sound director → Refiner)

### Findings

## Economy and pacing
- B1 (must-fix): Every swept cell is reset to h=0 + 1.5 s rest, including grass still below the 0.3 yield height. Regrowth to 0.3 takes 12.5 s on field 15 (g 0.8), 7.4 s on field 1. A brisk methodical player returns before 0.3, resets every cell and earns nothing: serpentine on 34×34, k 2.4, g 0.8: phone (brush 1.8 cells) at 40 cells/s ≈ 400 px/s → no win in 1000 s; desktop 30 cells/s → 519 s; solver "average" 14 cells/s → 61 s (wins only because slow). Random scribbler gets slower as it speeds up (87 s at 30 c/s, 112 s at 50). Suggest: grass below a flatten height (try 0.5) only bends and is not reset (keeps growing). With that: 49 s / 54 s / 61 s. DoD: "rubbing one spot earns nothing until it's above 0.5 again". Add fast-methodical and random-scribble solver profiles that must win within ~1.5× par.
- P2 (should-fix): Yield `v = clamp((h−0.3)/0.7)` but tufts draw "standing" from h≥0.15 → a player's second lap runs over visibly standing grass that gives no motes; best play is to wait. Suggest yield follows what you see (`v = h` or `h^1.5`, no threshold; the rest + logistic from seed already kills spot-rubbing), motes scale with yield; or if a threshold stays, the standing pose starts at the same height. (Reconcile with B1.)
- P3/B2 (should-fix): Starting k and time targets disagree ~3-4×. Swath ≈3-3.6 cells; field 1 at k 1.0 won in ~3-5 s (target 20-40 s; fails the 15 s unit test); field 15 at k 2.4 ~60-70 s (target 2.5-3.5 min). Hitting targets needs k ~2.5-3 (f1) and 5-8 (f15) = 5-8 identical laps per field, mostly waiting or monotonous. Suggest targets in laps not minutes: field 1 ~1.5 laps (10-15 s, tutorial) rising to ~3 laps on field 15 (~75-100 s); whole run ~15-20 min; late-field length from what changes between laps (bugs, stiff, stripes), not k. Brush minimum in px, e.g. radius max(0.75 cell, 18 px touch / 14 px mouse) — on desktop field 1 (60 px cells) the 1.5-cell minimum makes the mouse brush ~180 px wide, a broom not a hand. Write chosen lap counts into the tuning table; the brief's "1 to 3 minutes" was for the old model (update the brief's session-length line if changed).
- B2 (should-fix): Units: speed factor, solver profiles ("calm 8 cells/s", "average 14") and bug speeds are in cells/s; cells range ~10 px (f15 phone) to ~60 px (f1 desktop). Put speed factor and solver profiles in CSS px/s: calm ~150, average ~350, fast-methodical ~600, scribble; run the solver at real cell sizes for 390×844 and 1280×800 per field (bug size, anger radius and brush in cells all depend on layout). Flee is 1.4× species speed for 2 s = ~14 px on phone, still inside the 40 px anger radius → re-angers at once; make flee go until 1.5× anger radius away (px), with a 2 s cool-down before re-angering.
- P1/B3 (should-fix): Moves cost meter, earn nothing, and reset full-height grass that would have paid → double loss; the only payoff is the optional whole-field badge (which happens anyway).
